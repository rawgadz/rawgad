Installation Guide: How to Fit Your Cover Perfectly

Installing a steering wheel cover in the humidity of Bangladesh can be a bit tricky because the materials can be stiff. Follow these steps for a professional fit:

  1. Clean the Wheel: Use a microfiber cloth to remove all dust from your Toyota's steering wheel.
  2. Warm it Up: If the cover feels too tight, leave it on your dashboard under the sun for 15 minutes. This makes the material more pliable.
  3. Top-Down Approach: Start at the top (12 o'clock position) and work your way down the sides.
  4. The Final Stretch: The bottom will be the hardest part. Use your thumbs to pry the cover over the edge of the wheel.
  5. Alignment: Once it's on, rotate it until the stitching is perfectly centered.

Maintaining Your Steering Cover in Bangladesh

The dust levels in cities like Dhaka and Gazipur are high. To ensure your Rawgadz cover lasts for years:
